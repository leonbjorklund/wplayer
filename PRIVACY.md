# WPlayer Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

WPlayer has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or telemetry.

## Local processing and storage

WPlayer processes media-session and per-app volume information locally. It is not sent to the developer.

Settings and diagnostic logs are stored on the device and protected by Windows user-account permissions. Direct installations use `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WPlayer`; Microsoft Store installations use Windows-managed app storage. Logs are not uploaded automatically.

## Update checks

Direct installations contact GitHub at startup to check for updates. GitHub may receive standard connection data, such as the user's IP address, under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Microsoft Store installations do not contact GitHub for updates.

## Data removal

Uninstalling WPlayer removes its local data. The developer does not receive or retain a remote copy.
